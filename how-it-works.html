HOW IT WORKS

Simple Steps to Transformation

Getting started with Steryl is straightforward. Our proven process ensures minimal disruption and maximum impact for your institution.

01

Institution Onboarding

We start with a comprehensive assessment of your institution's needs, current workflows, and supply requirements.

WHAT HAPPENS IN THIS PHASE:

Initial consultation and needs assessment
Current inventory and workflow audit
Custom solution design
Stakeholder alignment
02

Digital Setup

Our team configures your digital infrastructure, migrates existing data, and sets up integrations with your systems.

WHAT HAPPENS IN THIS PHASE:

Platform configuration and customization
Data migration and validation
User account setup and access control
Integration with existing systems
03

Supply & Workflow Activation

We activate your supply chain, train your team, and launch your optimized procurement and inventory workflows.

WHAT HAPPENS IN THIS PHASE:

Initial supply orders and delivery
Staff training and certification
Workflow automation activation
Go-live support and monitoring
04

Ongoing Optimization

Continuous improvement through data analytics, regular reviews, and proactive supply chain management.

WHAT HAPPENS IN THIS PHASE:

Performance analytics and reporting
Quarterly business reviews
Predictive inventory management
Continuous platform updates
