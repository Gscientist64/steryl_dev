How It Works

Order to doorstep in 3 steps

Step 1

Facility Submits Order

A department head selects products from the verified catalogue, sets quantities, and submits — the request enters the system instantly.

Step 2

Steryl Verifies & Routes

The platform validates the order, runs checks against the manufacturer catalogue, and routes it to the Steryl distributor — no manual email or call.

Step 3

Delivered & Tracked

The distributor approves, dispatches and the facility tracks delivery in real time. Inventory updates automatically on receipt.
